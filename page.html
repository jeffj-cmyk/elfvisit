How it works

Three steps to a knock at the door.

  1. You tell us about your kids and the gift

    Pick a date window, add names and ages, and tell us what Santa is bringing. Hand us the gift, or set a budget and we'll shop and wrap it.

  2. You choose the stories

    Pick two favorites from our shelf, or leave your own books by the door. The elf will know the kids' names, and a couple of things they've done well this year.

  3. The elf arrives

    A knock, a jingle, and a very excited elf. Stories on the couch, a gift from Santa with a note, a photo with the family, and off to the next house.

Questions

Things parents ask us.

Will my kids believe it?

That's the whole job. The elf arrives in full North Pole gear, knows the kids by name, and never breaks character. For the believers it's magic; for the older skeptics it's still a very fun evening.

How does the gift work?

Most parents hand us the gift at the door (or leave it in a bag on the porch) a few minutes before the visit. We wrap it in North Pole paper and the elf delivers it with a note from Santa. If you'd rather we shop, give us a budget and a few hints.

What if my child is shy, or cries?

It happens, especially with the littlest ones. Our elves are patient and good at reading the room. They'll slow down, sit on the floor, and let the kids come to them. The stories usually do the rest.

Where do you go?

All across the East Valley. If you're a little outside it, write to us anyway and we'll see what the sleigh can do.

Can we book for a party or several families?

Yes. Tell us how many kids and we'll quote a longer visit with more stories and a gift for each child.

What about the dog?

Dogs are welcome at story time. Our elf is small, and some dogs get very excited about a stranger with bells on, so please have your dog leashed or held when she comes through the door and whenever they're in the same room. If your dog is a jumper, a few minutes in another room keeps everyone safe and story time calm.
